# Shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts: one global hotkey that opens the menu bar panel from anywhere, and single-key shortcuts that work while the panel is open.

**Header:** light-blue tile with a white ⌘, title "Shortcuts".
**Subtitle:** "Control {App} from your keyboard"

## Global Shortcut

Section icon: cyan ⌘.

One card with one row:

- Green tile with a tray and down-arrow glyph.
- Title "Open {App}", description "Show or hide the {App} menu from anywhere."
- On the right, a key recorder: a dark rounded field about 130 pt wide showing the current combination centred, "⌃⌥J" (Control-Option-J). Clicking it records a new combination.

## Menu Shortcuts

Section icon: purple keyboard.

One card with four rows separated by dividers, and a footer. Each row has a coloured tile, a title, a description, and a key recorder holding a single letter:

| Tile | Title | Description | Key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| blue circled "i" | Battery Information | Show or hide this section in the menu. | I |
| orange bolt in a circle | Apps Using Significant Energy | Show or hide this section in the menu. | U |
| teal devices glyph | Connected Devices | Show or hide this section in the menu. | D |
| yellow leaf | Low Power Mode | Turn Low Power Mode on or off. | L |

"Show or hide this section" means expand or collapse that section of the menu bar panel.

Footer, in small grey text under a divider: "Single keys work while the {App} menu is open."

Below the card, right-aligned, a grey text button: "Reset to default shortcuts". It restores the defaults shown above.
