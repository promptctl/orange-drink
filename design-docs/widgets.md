# Widgets

Previews the app's WidgetKit widgets and explains how to add and set them up. The widgets themselves are configured in the macOS widget editor, not here.

**Header:** sky-blue tile with a white 2×2 grid, title "Widgets".
**Subtitle:** "Put your batteries on the desktop and in Notification Center"

## Widget Types

Section icon: white 2×2 grid. On the far right of the section header, a small green dot with the green text "Live preview": the preview shows real current data.

One large card with:

**Type picker.** Three outlined capsule buttons, each with an icon. The selected one has a lighter fill and border.
- "All Batteries" (2×2 grid icon), selected.
- "Mac Battery" (laptop icon).
- "Device Battery" (earbuds icon).

**Size picker.** Text-only segmented pills below the type picker: "Small" (selected, dark grey fill), "Medium", "Large".

**Preview.** The selected widget at its real size, centred. The reference shows All Batteries at Small: a rounded square of about 140 pt with a dark fill and faint border, holding a 2×2 grid of battery rings. Each ring is:
- A circular track about 38 pt across, dark grey, with a light-grey arc filled clockwise from the top to the battery level.
- A device-type glyph in the centre: phone, wireless arcs, tablet, laptop.
- The percentage in bold below the ring.

The Mac's own ring is drawn in green with a small green lightning bolt at the top because it is charging; its percentage ("80%") is also green. The other rings are grey.

Below a divider, the card explains the selected type:

> Your Mac and all your devices in one widget. Small, medium, large and extra large: the bigger the size, the more devices fit.

Then a bold line, "What you can modify (right-click the widget → Edit Widget):", followed by four rows, each with a small grey icon:
- **Devices:** Automatic (as many as fit), Custom (your order), or Same as menu bar
- **Style:** Rings, List or Bars
- Show device names · Include this Mac
- **Appearance:** Color or Monochrome

### The three widget types

- **All Batteries:** the Mac plus every device. Sizes small, medium, large, and extra large.
- **Mac Battery:** the Mac alone.
- **Device Battery:** a single device, chosen in the widget's settings.

The reference only shows All Batteries in detail. The other two use the same type and size pickers.

## Add a Widget

Section icon: white square with a plus, layered over another square.

One card with three numbered steps separated by dividers. Each step starts with a cyan circle holding a white number:

1. "Right-click your desktop and choose **Edit Widgets…**, or open Notification Center (click the clock) and scroll to the bottom."
2. "Search for **{App}** and drag a widget wherever you like. Each size is its own entry in the gallery."
3. "Right-click a placed widget → **Edit Widget** to choose devices, style and names. Click a widget to open {App}."

Clicking a placed widget opens the app.
