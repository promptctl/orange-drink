# iPhone & iPad

Battery health, cycle count, and lifetime stats for an iPhone or iPad connected to the Mac. The reference shows only the empty state, before any device is connected.

**Header:** indigo tile with a laptop screen and a phone, title "iPhone & iPad".
**Subtitle:** "Battery health, cycles, and lifetime stats for a connected iPhone or iPad."
**Top-right:** a grey circular-arrow refresh button on the subtitle line. It re-scans for devices.

## Empty state

Everything is centred horizontally in the upper half of the content area.

1. An indigo phone outline with two curved "vibration" arcs on each side, about 64 pt tall.
2. The bold white heading "Connect your iPhone or iPad".
3. Grey text, two lines: "Plug it into your Mac with a cable, unlock it, and tap **Trust This Computer**." The button name is bold.
4. A card about 350 pt wide with three numbered steps. Each step starts with an indigo circle holding a white number, followed by a bold white title and a grey description:
   1. **Connect with a USB cable.** "Use the cable that came with your device."
   2. **Unlock & tap Trust.** "Your device asks to trust this Mac the first time."
   3. **It appears here.** "Health, cycles, and lifetime stats load automatically."
5. Below the card, grey text: "Prefer wireless? Turn on **"Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi"** in Finder, then check it over Wi-Fi." The reference limits the wireless option to a paid tier. Here it's available to everyone.

## Connected state

Not shown in the reference. Once a device is trusted, this screen replaces the empty state with that device's battery health percentage, cycle count, and lifetime stats. It presumably uses the same stat-tile and card layout as [battery.md](battery.md).
